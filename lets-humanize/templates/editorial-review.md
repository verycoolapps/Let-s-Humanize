# Humanization and Craft Review

Score each dimension from 1 (materially weak) to 5 (strong for this audience and task). Add evidence; do not grade by instinct alone.

## Rubric

- **Purpose:** Is the task, point, and next action clear?
- **Audience fit:** Does language, detail, and tone fit the actual reader and channel?
- **Specificity:** Are choices and examples tied to the real context?
- **Evidence:** Are claims, numbers, quotations, and sources traceable and qualified?
- **Structure:** Does information arrive in a useful order without repetition?
- **Craft:** Is wording, code, or visual hierarchy precise and deliberate?
- **Usability:** Are relevant accessibility, edge, error, and maintenance needs handled?
- **Integrity:** Is intent preserved, authorship transparent, and private information protected?

## Findings

For the highest-impact issues, record:

1. **Observation** — quote, location, behavior, or element.
2. **Impact** — what the intended reader/user loses or risks.
3. **Repair** — the concrete change.
4. **Verification** — the source, test, review, or user outcome that would confirm it.

## Final gate

- [ ] Fix the highest-risk correctness or integrity issue first.
- [ ] Fix structural problems before copy polish or cosmetic styling.
- [ ] Preserve strong material and the user's intended voice.
- [ ] Verify concrete details; label unsupported examples as hypothetical.
- [ ] Apply relevant domain checks, including accessibility or software tests.
- [ ] Do not optimize for detector evasion or claim guaranteed reception.
- [ ] Return the revised artifact or a short prioritized review—not a ritual scorecard unless requested.

### Score interpretation

Scores are a prioritization aid, not a universal quality certificate. A low score on a non-critical dimension may be acceptable; a single integrity, safety, accessibility, or correctness failure may still block release.
